How The Prototype Works

Read The Mountain

1. Each turn telegraphs a hazard: a rock pattern or an avalanche.
2. Choose a target lane, then choose one of your five grammar slots.
3. A correct answer triggers the slot bonus, and the movement phase resolves.
4. Weak swings dodge 1 lane. Strong swings dodge any lane and hard-evade avalanches.
5. Snow and ash dirty the lens. Use Klare Sicht before the screen becomes unreadable.
